# Manny's knockback: design spec (v0.8.0)

> **On hold (owner, 2026-10-30).** Manny-specific work is paused while the game's overall visual
> direction is settled ([art/VISUAL_DIRECTION.md](art/VISUAL_DIRECTION.md)). Everything here is kept as
> decided; no further development time until it's resumed.

Status:
- **Mechanic approved by the owner** (2026-10-30): enemies move backward along their actual route,
  with fixed tile distances, weight resistance and diminishing returns. The simulation stays
  deterministic, and the airborne flight is drawing only.
- **Numbers provisional**, for testing.
- **Not in the game.** The rules live in `src/balance/manny/model.ts` (pure, unit-tested in
  `tests/mannyModel.test.ts`). Results are in [balance/manny.md](balance/manny.md), regenerated by
  `tests/manny.report.ts`. The battle-screen prototype uses the same rules.

## 1. Attacks: punches and a charged launch

Not every punch launches. Manny has two attacks:

| | Normal punch | Charged launch ("haymaker") |
| --- | --- | --- |
| When | Every attack, except each 3rd | Every 3rd attack (two punches charge it) |
| Damage | Modest, per target | ×1.5 punch damage |
| Knockback | None | Backward along the route (section 2) |
| Targets | Up to the level's target count, first along the route first | Same |

Both attacks have a 0.25 s wind-up. Targets are chosen when the blow lands, within 1.25 tiles of
Manny's tile, so an enemy that walks out of reach during the wind-up is missed. That matters against
fast enemies.

**Provisional levels** (the owner's values; punch damage is my proposal):

| Level | Punch damage | Attack interval | Targets | Launch knockback (light enemy) | Daze after landing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 18 | 3.2 s | 1 | 0.75 tiles | none |
| 2 | 26 | 2.7 s | up to 2 | 1.75 tiles | 0.15 s |
| 3 | 40 | 2.3 s | up to 3 | 3.0 tiles | 0.3 s |

Intent:
- **Level 1:** a weak, slow melee fighter that struggles against crowds.
- **Level 2:** a useful control unit.
- **Level 3:** powerful and satisfying, without locking enemies near the spawn.

## 2. Distance

Measured in **tiles of route distance**. A percentage of the route would scale with map length and
break multi-map balance.

```
landing  = max(routeStart, progress − distance)
distance = knockTiles[level] × weightFactor[enemy] × repeatFactor[enemy]
           (then capped at 12% of the route length)
```

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `weightFactor` | light 1.0 (Goblin, Wolf) · medium 0.7 · heavy 0.45 (Armored Orc) · boss 0.2 (Ogre Warlord) |
| `repeatFactor` | 1st knock 1.0 · 2nd 0.6 · 3rd 0.35 · then 0.2; resets after 6 s without a knock |
| Route cap | 12% of the route per knock (4.6 tiles on Switchback; never reached by these levels) |

On Switchback (a 38-tile route), level 3's 3 tiles is 7.9%, so 60% becomes about 52%.

## 3. States and timing

```
walking ──launch──▶ airborne ──land──▶ dazed ──▶ walking
                      │                  │
                untargetable       targetable, not moving
```

| State | Duration | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Airborne** | 0.2 s + 0.1 s per tile knocked (0.28 s at level 1) | Progress jumps to the landing point at once (the simulation's truth). Not targetable or damageable, can't leak. Homing projectiles retarget or fizzle. |
| **Dazed** | the level's daze × weight (light 1, medium 0.8, heavy 0.5, boss 0) | On the road, targetable, not moving. |
| **Boss stagger** | 0.15 s, no flight | Bosses skid back the reduced distance and flinch. |

**Airborne and daze are counted.** The balance report includes them in the time each knock costs. At
level 1 (no daze) a goblin loses 0.78 s per launch: 0.5 s walking back plus 0.28 s in the air.

## 4. Edge cases

- **Corners:**
  - The flight is a straight arc between two route points, crossing the grass at bends.
  - The arc height is at least ~40 px, so even a tiny hop around a U-bend reads as a launch.
  - The shadow follows the arc's ground line.
- **Several enemies:**
  - Each gets its own distance (weight and repeat history), so groups keep their order.
  - Landings are staggered by 0–80 ms in the drawing only, and the existing sideways spread prevents
    stacking.
- **Route start:** the landing clamps there.
- **Speeds:** the same distance costs fast enemies less time; that's intended.
- **Wind-up misses:** fast enemies can walk out of reach during the 0.25 s wind-up. The report counts
  these as misses.
- **Determinism:** the rules run on the fixed simulation step with no randomness. Tumble, landing
  jitter and hit-stop are drawing only.
- **Events:** `enemyKnocked {enemy, fromProgress, toProgress, airTime}` and `enemyLanded`.

## 5. Results summary (full tables in [balance/manny.md](balance/manny.md))

| | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 | Archer L1 / L3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Damage per second, one target | 6.6 | 11.2 | 20.3 | 14.0 / 46.8 |
| Damage per second, all targets hit | 6.6 | 22.5 | 60.9 | 14.0 / 46.8 |
| Goblins leaked, wave 1, corner (of 20) | 20 | 16 | 2 | 17 / 0 |
| Goblins leaked, wave 5, corner | 20 | 20 | 16 | 19 / 1 |
| Mixed wave 5 leaked, corner (of 16) | 16 | 16 | 9 | 15 / 3 |
| Time lost per goblin, wave 10, corner | 0.04 s | 0.34 s | 0.84 s | — |
| Longest time in the first 3 tiles, spawn placement (goblin needs 2.0 s unhindered) | 2.7 s | 3.6 s | 3.5 s | — |

What it shows:
- **Level 1 is weak, as intended.** It does half an Archer L1's damage and lets nearly everything
  through. Its 0.75-tile launch every 9.6 s barely slows anything. The air time adds 0.28 s per
  launch, so it doesn't make level 1 strong.
- **Level 2 is modest.** Two targets and a 1.75-tile launch every 8.1 s gives about 0.25–0.35 s of
  delay per enemy. That's a mild control unit, perhaps less "useful" than intended.
- **Level 3 is strong against crowds.** Its 60.9 damage per second when hitting three beats an
  Archer L3, and it kills most early goblins at a corner. Against a single target it is weaker than an
  Archer L3.
- **There's no spawn lock at any level.** The worst case near the spawn is 3.5–4.8 s in the first three
  tiles, against 2–4 s unhindered.
- **Alone, no hero holds late waves.** From wave 10, a single Manny or Archer L3 lets almost everything
  through; the game needs several towers.
- **Wind-up misses against wolves.** At wave 1 on a straight stretch, level 3 lets more wolves through
  than level 2 (16 against 13). The wolf leaves Manny's short reach during the wind-up.

## 6. Open questions

1. **Level 2's role.** Launching on every 2nd attack instead of every 3rd roughly doubles the time
   bought per enemy (report section 4b): at wave 10 a level-2 Manny holds each enemy 0.43 s instead of
   0.23 s. Should level 2 or 3 charge faster?
2. **Wolves.** A longer reach (1.5 tiles), or choosing targets when the wind-up starts, would make
   Manny fair against fast enemies.
3. **Cost.** At level 1 Manny is weaker than a 50-gold Archer, so his price should reflect that.
   Proposal: 60 gold, then 70 and 120 for the upgrades, to be checked with the full balance harness
   once he's in the game.

## 7. Tests

**Done** (`tests/mannyModel.test.ts`):
- the distance, weight, repeat and route-start clamp;
- the route cap;
- the boss stagger with no flight, and no daze at level 1;
- every enemy type has a weight;
- placements;
- only every 3rd attack launches, within the target cap;
- determinism and staying on the route;
- a level-1 Manny lets a crowd through.

**When it moves into the game:**
- airborne enemies untargetable and unable to leak;
- projectiles retarget;
- timings at 2× and 3×;
- fuzz invariants;
- an end-to-end test where a launched enemy lands on the road and walks on.
