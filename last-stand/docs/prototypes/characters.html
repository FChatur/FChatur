Last Stand Character Studies

Design explorations for three heroes. Each starts from a personality and a few memorable hooks, then a silhouette, expressions and signature poses. Everything here is vector art drawn in code as concept work, not final in-game art. Judge the designs; final polish would come from the art pipeline described in the last section.

At gameplay size (about 90 px tall), next to today's Wren

What this art is, and what polished art needs

  • These drawings are flat-shaded vector art written in code. That is enough to settle a design: silhouette, proportions, costume, colours and expressions.
  • The rich painted shading, texture and fine line quality of a polished commercial game come from a dedicated illustrator. Code can approximate them, not match them.
  • Approved designs can go to an illustrator as layered artwork (one layer per body part) and be animated in the engine, or be delivered as finished animation sheets. The engine side is the same either way.
