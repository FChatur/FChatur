Manny design lab

Stage 1: settle one excellent, consistent Manny before any animation. Add candidate images, compare them at the size players will actually see (128 px tall on the 1600 × 900 screen), as silhouettes, at phone scale and against busy backgrounds, score them against the design lock, then check the favourite holds together in other poses.

1. Generate candidates

In ChatGPT, attach the concept sheet (docs/art/reference/concept-sheet-v1.webp) and use one of these directions. Two or three images per direction is plenty. Download the biggest PNG; transparent if offered.

2. Candidates

Each image is cut out (transparent, or a flat background removed) and trimmed. Scores and notes are saved with the page.

3. Consistency check (favourite)

Generate the favourite in two or three other poses (attach it and say "the exact same character, now …": a back three-quarter view, a punch, a victory pose). If the hair, fists, belt or proportions drift here, they will drift in every animation frame. The overlay shows the favourite and a pose on top of each other: matching shapes and colours stay dark, changes glow. The flicker view swaps them twice a second.
