# Phase 7 / v0.7.0 design proposal: maps, stars and Freeplay (GitHub issue #4)

Status: **proposal for the owner's approval**. Nothing here is implemented in the game. Mockups and map
previews are generated by `npm run art` (`art-preview/map-*.png`) and `docs/mockups/progression.html`.

## 1. Decisions needed

| # | Decision | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D1 | How wave 50 becomes reachable (today no bot strategy clears it) | Gentler enemy-health growth after wave 25, steeper again in Freeplay (section 2) |
| D2 | Starter roster | Four maps, one per tier; Switchback becomes Intermediate (section 3) |
| D3 | Unlock rule | Clear any map of the previous tier (section 4) |
| D4 | Star rule | Based on lives *lost*, exact integer boundaries (section 5) |
| D5 | Order at wave 50 | Victory screen first; the wave-50 upgrade pick only if you continue (section 6) |
| D6 | Menu flow | Main menu "Play" opens map select; it remembers your last map |

| D8 | Browser tests reaching wave 50 | A test-only build flag; never in the deployed game (section 9) |

| D10 | Art direction | Illustrated 2D with cutout rigs, vertical slice first ([ART_DIRECTION.md](ART_DIRECTION.md)) |
| D11 | Order of work | Art direction and free placement first, then maps and progression (section 12) |

## 2. Evidence: can wave 50 be cleared? (D1)

The balance bots (6 strategies × 3 seeds, the same ones `npm run balance` uses) were run to wave 50 on
Switchback and three candidate maps. Stars use the rule in section 5. Only enemy health growth per wave
(`hpGrowthPerWave`) was varied; everything else is today's balance.

| Map (tier) | Growth 12%/wave (today) | 10%/wave | 9%/wave |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Millbrook Meadows (Beginner) | 0/18 cleared (best: wave 50, lost) | 15/18 cleared · stars 2/7/6 (1★/2★/3★) | 15/18 · 0/1/14 |
| Switchback (Intermediate) | 0/18 (best: wave 44) | 3/18 · 1/2/0 | 15/18 · 3/6/6 |
| Ridge Pass (Advanced) | 0/18 (best: wave 40) | 1/18 · 1/0/0 | 12/18 · 0/9/3 |
| The Gauntlet (Expert) | 0/18 (best: wave 41) | 2/18 · 1/1/0 | 12/18 · 2/8/2 |

Also on Switchback at today's growth: scaling all enemy health down by 20% or 35% clears nothing; at 50%,
only the Mixed strategy clears, with 1 star.

Conclusions:
- With today's curve a wave-50 target is out of reach for every scripted strategy, on every map.
- A per-map health modifier (scaling all waves) is the wrong tool: it makes waves 1–30 trivial before
  wave 50 becomes fair.
- The late-game *slope* decides it. Around 10% growth the four maps line up into clear tiers:
  Beginner is comfortable, Intermediate is hard, and Advanced and Expert need strong play.

**Recommended (D1, option A):**
- Waves 1–25 are unchanged.
- From wave 26 to the completion wave, growth eases so that wave-50 enemies are about as tough as in the
  10% run.
- Freeplay (after the completion wave) climbs steeply again.
- Each map is then fine-tuned with its own modifiers, so Expert ends clearly harder than Advanced.
- During implementation this is validated with the harness, and its report gains "cleared / stars"
  columns per map.

The bots are simple; people play better. The targets below assume that:
- **Beginner:** most sensible builds clear it, good ones with 3 stars.
- **Intermediate:** good builds clear it.
- **Advanced:** needs a deliberate plan.
- **Expert:** 3 stars is a real feat.

Alternatives:
- **B. Per-map completion waves** (e.g. 30/40/45/50) with today's curve. No balance change, but the issue
  asks for wave 50 as the standard, and stars would mean different things per map.
- **C. Per-map health modifiers only.** No curve change, but the early game becomes trivial (see above).

## 3. Map roster (D2)

Four maps, one per tier, so the progression is complete from day one. The tier screen is built for many
maps per tier. Difficulty comes from geometry and terrain, not from inflated health.

| Map (stable ID) | Tier | Road | Build tiles | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Millbrook Meadows (`millbrook`) | Beginner | 59 tiles, 6 bends | 181 | A long serpentine road: enemies are in range for a long time and every spot covers two lanes. Forgiving; teaches coverage. |
| Switchback (`switchback`) | Intermediate | 37 tiles, 6 bends | 203 | Today's map, unchanged. Fewer overlapping lanes: you must pick your corners. |
| Ridge Pass (`ridge-pass`) | Advanced | 31 tiles, 4 bends | 181 | A short road; cliffs fill the inside of the bends, so the corner spots (the best ones) are gone. Range and the Ballista matter. |
| The Gauntlet (`gauntlet`) | Expert | 25 tiles, 2 bends | 84 | A lake and a rocky outcrop leave few ledges. Every tower spot is a choice; slowing (Frost) and focus fire decide it. |

New engine features this needs (all small and data-driven):
- Maps gain a `tier` and **blocked areas** (`water` or `cliff`): tile blocks in the previews above,
  shapes once free placement (section 11) lands. Nobody can build there, the placement preview says why,
  and the terrain painter draws lakes and rocky outcrops.
- Each map keeps its own modifiers.
- Blocked tiles also feed the bots' spot ranking, so the balance tooling works on any map, as the
  balance principles require.

Deferred (D7): **Twin Gates**, two entrances that merge. It's a strong Expert idea, but enemies would need
per-enemy routes in the simulation, rendering and bots. I'd rather do it properly in a later update.

## 4. Unlock rules (D3)

- Beginner maps are always open.
- A tier opens when you clear (≥1★) **any** map in the previous tier. A tier with no maps yet is
  skipped, so a requirement can never point at something that doesn't exist.
- **Existing players** keep access: any map with a saved record from before v0.7.0 stays open. That
  covers Switchback for current players.
- A locked card says exactly what opens it ("Clear any Advanced map").
- Why not star totals (e.g. "12★ to open Expert")? They read well, but a hard map can block a player who
  is good enough. Star totals can come back later as a bonus (achievements in v0.8.0).

## 5. Stars (D4)

Stars are decided once per run, the moment the completion wave (default 50, set per map) is cleared:

| Stars | Condition (S = starting lives, L = lives lost this run, R = S − L) |
| --- | --- |
| ★★★ | L = 0 (no lives lost) |
| ★★ | 2·R ≥ S (at least half left; with 20 lives, 10 left still counts) |
| ★ | R ≥ 1 (cleared) |
| none | Lost before clearing the completion wave |

- **Lives lost, not lives left**, so a future effect that restores lives can't buy stars back. All
  arithmetic is integer, with no rounding questions. For an odd S, half means rounding up (S = 15 needs 8).
- **The best result is kept per map** (stars never go down). Best wave is tracked separately.
- **Freeplay never changes stars.**

## 6. Completion and Freeplay flow (D5)

1. Wave 50 is cleared.
2. Completion, stars and best wave are **saved immediately**, before anything is shown.
3. Heroes celebrate (the boss-wave cheer), then the victory screen appears:
   - stars revealed one by one (instantly with reduced motion);
   - lives, kills, towers and gold;
   - any map just unlocked.
4. The game is halted behind this screen: Auto Wave's countdown is suspended and pause is disabled.
   Then you choose:
   - **Claim Victory:** the run ends and you return to map select.
   - **Continue Freeplay:** the wave-50 upgrade pick appears (it was going to anyway), then wave 51
     follows under the normal Auto Wave rules. The HUD shows a FREEPLAY chip and the next milestone.
5. In Freeplay:
   - waves continue with no end and get harder each wave;
   - milestones at waves 75, 100, 150, 200, then every 100, each with a toast;
   - the best Freeplay wave is saved after every cleared wave, so closing the tab loses nothing.
6. Freeplay ends at defeat. The results screen shows the wave reached, the new best and the unchanged
   stars, with options Retry or Maps.
7. Each run is awarded once (a per-run flag). Restarting starts a new run: a free Wren again, and the
   completion flag reset.
8. A loss before wave 50 shows the defeat screen: the wave reached against the target, your best, and
   what the next star needs.

Freeplay stays bounded: enemies per wave are already capped at 30 (plus a boss), so performance doesn't
grow with the wave number. Health values stay far from number limits for any reachable wave. Big
numbers on screen get compact formatting (12.4k, 3.1M).

## 7. Map selection screen (mockups)

- **Desktop:** the four tiers side by side, each with its map cards:
  - each card shows a painted preview, name, stars, best wave and a CLEARED ribbon;
  - locked cards are greyed with a padlock and the requirement;
  - a detail panel shows the selected map: big preview, a short "how this map plays" note, road length,
    build tiles, best wave and the three star goals;
  - **Play** and **Back** buttons.
  - A **next goal** banner always names one concrete aim ("earn ★★ on Switchback").
- **Mobile:** tier tabs and a single list of cards with a Play button on each card; nothing relies on
  hover.
- **Keyboard:** arrow keys move between cards (locked ones too, so the requirement can be read), Enter
  plays, Esc goes back.
- **Previews:** drawn by the same terrain painter as the game. On hover or focus a goblin walks the
  road; with reduced motion they stay still.

## 8. Technical approach

- **Stable IDs:** `millbrook`, `switchback`, `ridge-pass`, `gauntlet`. IDs are never renamed (saves key
  on them); display names can change freely.
- **Config:** `MapConfig` gains `tier`, `blocked`, and `completionWave` (default 50). The late-game curve
  and the Freeplay curve live in `config/balance.ts`; the maps stay pure data.
- **Core:**
  - `Simulation` counts lives lost and knows the completion wave.
  - It emits `mapCompleted` (once per run), `freeplayMilestone` and `runEnded`. Blocked tiles give a new
    placement reason, `terrain`.
- **Progress:** pure, unit-tested functions in a new `game/progress.ts`: stars, unlocks, the next goal
  and migration.
- **Saves:** a new `lastStand.v2.progress` key holds a format version, per-map entries (stars,
  completed, best wave, best Freeplay wave, best kills, runs) and the last played map.
  - **Migration** reads the v1 records once. Best wave, kills and runs carry over.
  - A v1 best wave of 51 or more proves wave 50 was cleared with lives left, so it becomes "completed, 1★".
    Nothing else is guessed.
  - The v1 key is left untouched, so rolling back to 0.6.0 still works.
  - Corrupt or blocked storage falls back to defaults, as today.
- **Achievements later (v0.8.0):** the events above, plus the saved progress, are what an achievement
  system needs ("Flawless Victory" = a `mapCompleted` with 3 stars; "Centurion" = a Freeplay milestone
  at 100). No achievement code ships in v0.7.0.
- **Unchanged:** the free starter Wren, resale rules, targeting, the heroes, the shop and the upgrade
  draft.

## 9. Tests

- **Unit tests:**
  - star boundaries (S = 20 and odd S, lives lost 0/1/10/11/19);
  - best stars never decrease;
  - Freeplay never changes stars;
  - completion is awarded exactly once per run;
  - best Freeplay wave is saved per cleared wave;
  - unlock rules, including empty tiers and grandfathered maps;
  - migration from v1 (including bestWave ≥ 51), plus corrupt and partial saves;
  - blocked tiles refuse building;
  - the late and Freeplay curves are continuous at waves 25 and 50;
  - free Wren and resale are unchanged.
- **Browser tests:**
  - pick a map;
  - reach and clear wave 50;
  - see the stars;
  - unlock the next tier;
  - continue into Freeplay;
  - claim victory;
  - reload and check progress;
  - locked cards can't be played;
  - a mobile-size smoke test.
  - Reaching wave 50 in a browser test needs a shortcut. I propose a test-only build flag (D8) that allows
    jumping to a wave. It is compiled out of the deployed game.
- **Balance and performance:** harness report with stars per map; wave-50 and Freeplay-wave-150
  benchmarks.

## 10. Out of scope for v0.7.0

New towers or enemies, achievements, a tutorial rework, multi-entrance maps, audio.

## 11. Bloons-style free placement (D9)

Towers can go anywhere they fit, not on a tile grid. Mockup: a Millbrook layout with towers at free
positions, a valid ghost with its range, and a refused one.

**Rules:**
- Each tower has a round **footprint**: about 0.45 tiles across for Wren, Fen and Old Rime, and 0.65
  for Brakk's wider machine.
- A spot is valid when the footprint:
  - is inside the map;
  - keeps clear of the road (distance from the road's centre line ≥ half the road's width + footprint);
  - stays off blocked areas (lakes, cliffs);
  - doesn't overlap another tower's footprint. Footprints may touch.
- The preview names the reason a spot is refused: "Too close to the road", "Overlaps Wren", "On water",
  "Not enough gold".

**Simulation:**
- Towers keep continuous positions (they already have `x`, `y`); placement checks become geometry
  instead of tile lookups.
- Selecting a tower hit-tests its footprint and sprite.
- Range, targeting, the free Wren and resale are unchanged.

**Input:**
- The ghost follows the pointer smoothly (click-to-place and drag-and-drop both stay).
- While placing, the road's no-build band is tinted, so the rules are visible.
- On touch screens, the ghost sits above the finger.
- With the keyboard, arrow keys nudge the ghost a quarter tile and Enter places it.

**Maps and bots:**
- Blocked areas become shapes (polygons) instead of tile lists.
- Bots rank candidate spots on a quarter-tile lattice by road coverage, keeping only valid ones, so the
  balance tooling works on any map.

**Balance:**
- Tighter packing near corners than a 1-tile grid allows makes the game somewhat easier.
- Footprint sizes are the dial: the harness compares grid against free placement before any value is
  set.

**Size and risk:** medium. It touches the core, the placement preview, input, bots and tests (geometry
unit tests and browser tests), but not the art.

## 12. Implementation order (D11)

Recommended: change the look and the feel first, then build maps and progression on top. That way the
maps, map previews, cards and victory screens are drawn once, in the final style.

1. **Free placement** in the core, input, preview and bots, on the current art. Testable on its own.
2. **Art direction vertical slice** (if B): rig pipeline, Wren, the Goblin, Millbrook terrain and hit
   effects, in the game behind a setting. **Owner review on a hosted preview.**
3. **Convert the rest:** all heroes and levels, enemies and the boss, effects, shop portraits and the
   tower panel.
4. **Maps:** data model, shape-based terrain in the new style, the four starter maps, harness support.
5. **Progression:**
   - map select;
   - stars and saves (with migration);
   - victory and defeat screens;
   - Freeplay;
   - the late-game curve (D1), validated with the harness.
6. **Polish:** mobile, keyboard, reduced motion; benchmarks (including software rendering); a hosted
   preview; **owner approval**; release.

Version suggestion:
- v0.7.0 = steps 1–3 (the new look and free placement);
- v0.8.0 = steps 4–6 (maps, stars, Freeplay).

Achievements and new troops each move one version later.

Alternative: progression first on today's pixel art (steps 1, 4, 5 as v0.7.0), art afterwards. Players
get maps sooner, but the map art, previews and progression screens get redrawn later.

Each step ends with tests passing and, for anything visible, screenshots. Nothing is merged to `main`
without the owner's approval.
